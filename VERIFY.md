## Other problems

1. **Words screen overlaps on short phones (FAIL).** `#wordsScroll` is a fixed-height `display:grid` with `overflow:auto`; with the default `align-content:stretch` and shrinkable rows the preview's row shrinks below the 240px card, so the date line and the "What did you see?" field draw over the preview. Seen at 375x667, 360x640, 320x568 and 390x420 (a keyboard-sized viewport). Fine at 390x844. See `verify2-words-375x667.png` and `verify2-words-short-390x420.png`. The page check does not look for this; add a check that no field overlaps `#wordsPreviewWrap` at 375x667.
2. **Postmark text is hidden under the stamp.** The stamp sits on top of the ring, so the "M" of the time and the last letters of the city are lost ("PHILADELP..." and "2:36 P"). See `verify2-zoom-04-C-stamp.jpg`. With a 28-character city the ring text is tiny.
3. **A loop that fills the photo gets a blurred fill that looks like a vignette.** Photo 03-C shows the railing ending in a vertical edge; 02-C and 05-C show dim, smeared sides. No stretched pixels, so this is polish, but it is the first thing a person sees when they draw big.
4. **Trace screen on landscape photos.** About two thirds of the canvas area is empty cream above and below the photo.
5. **Sample wording.** The sample says "I saw a dolphin." (city Legnaro), but the plan, the hero fallback aria-label and the words placeholder say "whale".
6. **Film look.** On dusk and dark photos (05, 08) it turns brown and muddy, and the grain is heavy; with long words the details line is about 15px on the square.
7. **Look screen at 390x844** has about 300px of unused space between "Edit words / Edit drawing" and the bottom bar; the preview could be larger.
8. **Moon icon** is 16 to 18px on Postcard, Polaroid, Film and Just the sky at 1080: readable, but the smallest mark on the card.
9. **Postcard story and square** show a lot of empty cream paper above and below the card (the story keeps y 0..270 and 1540..1920 free on purpose).
10. **In-app note** sits below the fold on a 360x640 landing, so a person may not see it before tapping "Photograph the sky".
11. **Video speed:** 8.1 s on this PC, over the plan's about 5 s.
12. **README and og.jpg** do not exist yet (main session, M5).
13. **Grey-sky photo 09 (twilight trees) with the Ink pen** was hard to see in round 1 and was not re-checked this round.
14. **Glow** is subtle on most photos; a bigger lift would show in a screenshot or video.

## Needs a real phone

Tick each item in the "Tested by hand" column.

- **Camera capture (iPhone Safari, Android Chrome).** Tap "Photograph the sky". The camera should open, and the photo should land on the trace screen in about a second, upright, not rotated.
- **Choose a photo and desktop paste.** On a phone, pick a photo from the gallery. On a computer, paste a photo onto the landing page.
- **Drawing feel with a real finger.** Trace a loop, a swoosh and a dot with a thumb. Look for lag, a line that jumps, accidental page scroll or zoom, and a loop that does not close cleanly. Check the line looks like a confident pen and not a scribble.
- **Save image to Photos (iPhone).** Tap "Save image". The share sheet should open, and Save Image should put it in Photos with a name like `look-up-philadelphia-2026-10-06.jpg`.
- **Android download.** Tap "Save image". The file should land in the Gallery or Downloads with the same name, and the done note should say where.
- **Share story video.** Tap "Share story" and wait. It should finish in about 5 s (this PC took 8.1 s) and show "Share now". Tap it, and the share sheet should open. Play it from WhatsApp and Instagram; it should be 5 s, look like the preview and keep the card and words inside the Instagram safe area.
- **Keyboard on the words screen (after the Words layout fix).** Tap each field. The card preview must not be covered, the field being typed in must stay visible above the keyboard, and Next must stay reachable.
- **Caveat and safe-area insets (iPhone).** The "I saw a whale." text should be handwriting, not a fallback font. Bars must clear the home indicator, and the top must clear the notch.
- **Instagram in-app browser.** Open a link to the site from a real Instagram DM or bio. The yellow "Opened inside another app?" note should show; check the camera, Save and Share story. If saving fails, the "Saving works best in your browser" sheet should open and "Copy the link" should copy the address.
- **HEIC from a real iPhone.** Pick a photo straight from the iPhone library and check it opens, or that the HEIC screen reads kindly.
- **12-hour and 24-hour time.** Set the phone to 24-hour. The date line and postmark should read like "19:42", and Film's stamp stays digits.
- **Dark-mode phone.** Turn on the phone's dark mode. The site should stay light, with no dark flash or unreadable text.
- **Slow data.** Throttle to 3G or use poor signal. The landing should be usable in a few seconds (about 453 KB uncompressed).
- **Animation.** Watch the preview loop on the look screen and the hero postcard on the landing: sky settles, line draws, words write, postmark stamps, then a pause. Check it is smooth, not choppy.
- **Analytics, README and og.jpg.** After the deploy, confirm page views appear in the Vercel dashboard, and that the README, the og:image (`assets/og.jpg`) and the live address exist.
